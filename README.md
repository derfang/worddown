# WordDown 📖
### *Intelligent, Multi-Modal English Vocabulary Acquisition*

[![Platforms](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platforms-Windows%20%7C%20Android-512BD4?style=for-the-badge&logo=windows&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/derfang/worddown/releases)
[![Offline First](https://img.shields.io/badge/Offline--First-100%25-success?style=for-the-badge&logo=icloud)](https://github.com/derfang/worddown)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green.svg?style=for-the-badge)](LICENSE)

**WordDown** is a fast, beautiful, offline-first English vocabulary learning application designed to help you master new words permanently. Instead of boring, dry flashcards, WordDown teaches words through **authentic context**: movie and speech video clips, real-world quotes, pronunciation in UK & US accents, common collocations, and an intelligent **Spaced Repetition System (SRS)** that reminds you right before you forget.

---

## ✨ Core Features

| Feature | What It Does for You |
|:---|:---|
| 🎬 **Authentic Video Clips** | Watch real-world video clips from movies, TV shows, and famous speeches with **synchronized subtitles** jumping directly to the moment the word is spoken. |
| 🧠 **Smart Spaced Repetition (SRS)** | Built on cognitive science (the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve). WordDown schedules reviews across an **11-stage memory ladder** ($1\text{ day} \to 1\text{ year}$), ensuring words move from short-term memory into permanent fluency. |
| 🎯 **8 Interactive Practice Modes** | Tests your active recall using 8 varied question formats: **Definition Matching, Quote Fill-in-the-Blank, Synonyms, Antonyms, Context Examples, Spelling & Misspelling Traps, Audio Listening, and Nuance Comparisons**. |
| ⚡ **Tap Any Word to Learn** | Every definition, quote, and example sentence is interactive! Tap any unknown word within a sentence to instantly jump to its full learning card. |
| 🎧 **Real Human Pronunciation** | Listen to authentic British (UK) and American (US) native pronunciations, backed by clear speech synthesis for full sentences and quotes. |
| 📚 **Curated Word Packs** | Over 50,000 words ranked by real-world usage frequency, plus dedicated curriculum tracks for **GRE, TOEFL, IELTS, 1500 Everyday Essentials, Idioms, Phrasal Verbs**, and specialized **Electrical Engineering & Electronics**. |
| 💾 **100% Offline with Cloud Sync** | Learn anywhere—even on an airplane or without mobile data. Sign in to effortlessly sync your progress across your **Windows PC** and **Android phone**. |

## 📥 Download & Installation

You don't need any programming tools or compilers to use WordDown! Ready-to-use release packages are provided for Windows and Android.

👉 **[Go to Latest Releases](https://github.com/derfang/worddown/releases)**

### 📱 Android Installation
1. On your Android device, go to **[Releases](https://github.com/derfang/worddown/releases)** and download **`app-release.apk`** (or download `WordDown_Android.zip` and extract it).
2. Tap the downloaded `.apk` file to install.
   *(If prompted by Android, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager).*
3. Open **WordDown** from your home screen and start learning!
> 💡 **Updating**: When a new version is released, simply download and install the new APK over the existing one. Your progress, learned words, and account login are safely preserved!

### 💻 Windows Installation
1. Go to **[Releases](https://github.com/derfang/worddown/releases)** and download **`WordDown_Windows.zip`**.
2. Right-click the `.zip` file and select **Extract All...** to a folder of your choice (e.g. `Documents\WordDown` or `Desktop\WordDown`).
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **`worddown_flutter.exe`** to launch the app!
> 💡 **Desktop Shortcut**: You can right-click `worddown_flutter.exe` $\to$ **Send to** $\to$ **Desktop (create shortcut)** for quick access.

## 🚀 How to Learn with WordDown

1. **Browse or Search**: Use the search bar to find any English word instantly, or choose a curated list from the **Curriculums** dropdown (such as *GRE*, *TOEFL*, or *1500 Essential Words*).
2. **Add to Queue**: Tap the **`+`** icon on words you want to study.
3. **Start Learning**: Tap the **Learn Now** banner to launch an active study session. WordDown introduces words through video clips, quotes, and meanings, then gives you immediate memory-retention checks.
4. **Daily Practice**: When words are due for review, the **Review Now** banner will notify you. Complete your quick 5-minute practice session to keep your retention streak alive!

## 📚 Curated Word Packs

WordDown organizes vocabulary by real-world usage and academic goals:

* 🎯 **1500 Essential Words**: The core high-frequency vocabulary needed for everyday conversation and reading.
* 🎓 **GRE Vocabulary**: Advanced academic and analytical words for graduate school examinations.
* 📝 **TOEFL Vocabulary**: Key academic vocabulary for university admissions and standardized testing.
* 🌐 **IELTS Vocabulary**: Essential band-boosting vocabulary for international English proficiency.
* 💬 **Idioms & Phrasal Verbs**: Natural figurative expressions, collocations, and conversational phrases.
* ⚡ **Electrical Engineering & Electronics**: Specialized technical domain terminology for engineering students and researchers.
